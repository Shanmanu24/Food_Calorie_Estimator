How it works About

Image recognition. Indian-food friendly.

Snap food.Know calories.

Upload a photo of your meal. Our AI names the dish and estimates its calories in seconds.

Loading the AI model… the first visit takes about a minute.

Estimates are approximate, for one typical serving.

Three steps. No typing.

  1. Upload your photo

    Pick a picture of your meal from your phone or computer.

  2. The AI names the dish

    The model runs in your browser and compares your photo with every dish in our list, including chapati, dosa and biryani.

  3. See the calories

    You get the calories for one typical serving, plus the next closest guesses. Tap a guess if the first one is wrong.

CalorieAI is a mini project. Calories are average values per serving, and real values change with oil, portion size and recipe.
